# Forge Physique Coaching — Onboarding & Client Journey

## Business Hours & Response Times

Forge Physique Coaching's official business hours are **Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM Central Time (Austin, Texas)**. These hours apply to guaranteed response times; Marcus often responds outside these hours as well, but clients should not expect it.

| Tier | Weekday Response Time | Weekend Response |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Within 48 hours | No weekend response guaranteed |
| Momentum | Within 24 hours | No weekend response guaranteed |
| Apex | Same business day | Monitored, but not guaranteed same-day |

No tier includes guaranteed weekend or holiday response. This is stated upfront during onboarding so expectations are clear from day one.

## Step 1 — Application

Every new client starts by completing an online application (a short form covering training history, goals, schedule, and equipment access). This is not a formality — Forge Physique Coaching reviews every application and does not accept clients who are not a realistic fit (see the "Who Forge Physique Coaching Serves" section of the Business Overview document).

## Step 2 — Discovery Call

Qualified applicants are invited to a free 20-minute discovery call (via Zoom or phone) with Marcus. This call covers:
- The client's specific goals and history
- An honest assessment of whether Forge Physique Coaching is the right fit
- Which tier makes the most sense given the client's needs and availability
- Any questions the prospective client has about the process

No pressure or same-call sign-up is used. Prospective clients are given up to 7 days to decide after the discovery call.

## Step 3 — Enrollment & Payment

Once a client decides to proceed:
1. Client selects their tier and completes payment (see Pricing & Payment Policy document)
2. Client receives a welcome email with login details for the coaching app
3. Client is added to the private client WhatsApp group/contact (Momentum and Apex tiers) or the check-in-only app flow (Foundation tier)

## Step 4 — Intake Process

Within 48 hours of enrollment, the client completes a detailed intake package inside the coaching app, covering:
- Full training history and current strength levels (or best estimates, for less experienced clients)
- Injury history and any current physical limitations
- Detailed schedule and equipment access
- Food preferences, allergies, and any dietary restrictions
- Starting photos (front, side, back) and starting weight
- Sleep, stress, and lifestyle factors that affect recovery

## Step 5 — First Program Delivery

Marcus builds the client's first training program and nutrition targets personally — this is never templated or automated. Delivery timeline:
- **Momentum & Apex tiers:** First program delivered within 3 business days of completed intake
- **Foundation tier:** First program delivered within 5 business days of completed intake

The first program is accompanied by a written or (Momentum/Apex) recorded video walkthrough explaining the reasoning behind the split, exercise choices, and nutrition targets.

## Step 6 — Ongoing Check-Ins

This is the core of the coaching relationship. Check-in cadence depends on tier (see Business Overview document), but the format is consistent:

1. Client submits: current weight, progress photos (weekly or bi-weekly depending on tier), training log/performance data, and a short written update on how the week felt (energy, adherence, any struggles)
2. Marcus reviews the submission and responds with written feedback, any program or nutrition adjustments, and answers to any questions raised
3. For Momentum and Apex tiers, this is supplemented by ongoing WhatsApp access for questions that come up between formal check-ins

## Step 7 — Monthly/Bi-Monthly Calls (Momentum & Apex only)

Momentum tier clients have one 20-minute video call per month; Apex tier clients have two per month. These calls are used for:
- Bigger-picture progress review (beyond what fits in a written check-in)
- Adjusting overall strategy if progress has stalled
- Addressing anything the client would rather discuss live than in writing

Foundation tier does not include video calls as a standard feature but may add a one-time consultation call at the hourly rate listed in the Pricing document.

| Channel | Purpose | Tiers |

## What Happens If a Client Goes Quiet

If a client misses two consecutive check-ins without notice, Marcus reaches out directly to check in. If there's no response after a third missed check-in, the client's spot is flagged as at-risk, and Marcus sends a direct, non-judgmental message asking whether the client wants to continue, pause, or needs the program adjusted. Forge Physique Coaching would rather have this conversation early than let a client quietly disengage and waste their own money.

## Progress Review Milestones

- **Week 4:** First full programming review — targets and split adjusted based on real data, not guesses
- **Week 12 (end of minimum commitment):** Full progress review — before/after comparison, decision point on continuing, adjusting tier, or transitioning to a maintenance-focused approach
- **Every 12 weeks thereafter:** Same full review cadence continues for the life of the coaching relationship

## Offboarding

Clients who complete their goals or choose to move on receive a final "maintenance guide" — a summary of the principles, targets, and training structure that got them their results, so they can continue independently. This reflects Forge Physique Coaching's stated philosophy of coaching clients toward independence rather than indefinite dependency.

## Month 1, Week by Week

To set clear expectations for new clients, here is exactly what the first month looks like:

- **Week 0 (Enrollment week):** Application review, discovery call, payment, welcome email, app access granted, intake package sent.
- **Week 1:** Intake package completed by client (typically takes 20–30 minutes); Marcus builds the first personalized program and nutrition targets; program delivered with a written or recorded walkthrough.
- **Week 2:** Client trains on the new program for the first time, logs all sessions; any early questions are addressed via WhatsApp (Momentum/Apex) or the app (Foundation).
- **Week 3:** Continued training and logging; Marcus monitors early logged data for any red flags (e.g., a movement consistently logged as painful, which triggers an early check-in regardless of the client's formal check-in schedule).
- **Week 4:** First formal check-in and full programming review — this is the first point where real trend data (not just starting estimates) informs adjustments to training volume and nutrition targets.

## Sample Check-In Submission Template

Every check-in, regardless of tier, asks the client to submit the same core fields, ensuring consistency and making trends easy to spot over time:

1. Current body weight (and the time of day it was taken, for consistency)
2. Progress photos — front, side, and back, taken in consistent lighting and clothing
3. Full training log for the period (exercises, weights, reps, and RPE for each working set)
4. Average nightly sleep hours for the period
5. Adherence self-rating (e.g., "hit my nutrition target 6 out of 7 days")
6. A short written note on how the week felt — energy, stress, any life events affecting training or eating
7. Any questions or concerns for Marcus to address

## Coaching App Features

The coaching app (used across all three tiers) includes:

- Program delivery with built-in exercise demonstration videos for every movement prescribed
- In-app workout logging (weight, reps, RPE) that auto-populates trend charts over time
- Nutrition target display with a built-in macro calculator reference
- Photo upload and side-by-side progress photo comparison tool
- Direct messaging thread with Marcus (used for Foundation tier's primary communication channel)
- Push notifications for check-in reminders and new program releases

## Holiday & Schedule Exceptions

Forge Physique Coaching observes the standard US federal holidays (New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving plus the following day, and Christmas Day through New Year's Day). During these periods, response times may extend beyond the normal guarantee by 1–2 business days; this is communicated to all active clients in advance via app notification each year. Programs and nutrition targets remain active and usable during holiday periods — only response times are affected.

## Full Intake Questionnaire Field List

For reference, the complete intake package clients fill out during Week 1 covers:

- Contact and account information
- Age, height, current weight, and general health status
- Full training history (years training, current program if any, self-assessed experience level)
- Current estimated strength levels on main lifts (or "unknown/beginner" if not applicable)
- Complete injury history, including past and current limitations
- Available training days per week and typical session length
- Equipment access (home gym, commercial gym, minimal equipment, or other)
- Occupation and general daily activity level outside of training
- Food preferences, dislikes, allergies, and any dietary restrictions or cultural/religious eating considerations
- Current eating pattern and any prior dieting history
- Typical sleep schedule and self-rated sleep quality
- Primary goal in the client's own words, plus any secondary goals
- Starting photos and starting weight
- Emergency contact information

## Success Milestones Beyond Week 12

- **Month 6:** A comprehensive review comparing the 6-month progress photos and strength data against the original starting point, alongside a conversation about whether the client's goal has evolved (e.g., from fat loss to a lean muscle gain phase) and adjusting the program's overall direction accordingly.
- **Month 12:** A full "year in review" — the most complete before/after comparison Forge Physique Coaching produces, along with a discussion of whether the client continues coaching, transitions to a lighter-touch maintenance tier, or graduates to full independence using their final maintenance guide.
