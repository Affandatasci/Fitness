# Forge Physique Coaching — Business Overview & Programs

## About Forge Physique Coaching

Forge Physique Coaching is an online physique and performance coaching business founded in 2021 by Marcus Cole, based in Austin, Texas, USA, and serving clients worldwide via remote coaching.

Forge Physique Coaching exists for one type of client: men who are done being average and are ready to commit to real, sustained physical transformation. The business does not run generic "get fit" programs. Every program is built around body recomposition, strength development, and the mindset work required to sustain results long after the program ends.

**Tagline:** "Built With Intention."

### Founding Story

Marcus Cole spent six years training clients in commercial gyms before moving fully online in 2021. The shift came after noticing a consistent pattern: clients who trained in person often lost momentum the moment the session ended, because nothing in their daily life outside the gym had actually changed. Online coaching, done properly, forces a different kind of accountability — it requires the client to own their nutrition, their consistency, and their mindset every single day, not just for the one hour they're being watched.

Forge Physique Coaching was built specifically to solve that gap: real coaching, real accountability, delivered entirely online, with the systems in place to catch a client before they fall off track rather than after.

### Mission

To give men a structured, honest path to the physique and discipline they want — without gimmicks, crash diets, or unsustainable extremes — and to build that transformation in a way that holds up long after the coaching relationship ends.

### Who Forge Physique Coaching Serves

Forge Physique Coaching works exclusively with men aged 22–45 who meet the following profile:

- Have tried "figuring it out alone" for at least a year with inconsistent results
- Are not looking for a quick fix or an 8-week transformation gimmick
- Can commit to a minimum 3-month coaching relationship
- Have access to either a home gym or a commercial gym membership
- Are willing to track their nutrition (not necessarily count every gram forever, but during the active coaching period)

Forge Physique Coaching does not currently work with:
- Clients under the age of 18
- Clients training for competitive bodybuilding shows requiring peak-week/contest prep protocols (this requires a different specialization and is referred out)
- Clients seeking coaching solely for a diagnosed eating disorder or clinical weight-management condition (these are referred to a registered dietitian or physician first)

### Brand Values

1. **No shortcuts, no gimmicks.** Every program is built on the same foundation — progressive overload, a sustainable calorie target, consistency over intensity.
2. **Radical honesty.** If a client isn't doing the work, they're told directly, not coddled.
3. **Systems over motivation.** Motivation fades. The coaching structure (check-ins, deadlines, accountability) is what keeps clients on track when motivation doesn't show up.
4. **Long-term thinking.** Every program is designed to be something the client can realistically sustain a version of for years, not just for the length of the coaching package.

## Coaching Programs

Forge Physique Coaching offers three coaching tiers, each described below. Full pricing for each tier is covered in the separate Pricing & Payment Policy document.

### Foundation Tier

The entry point for clients who want real coaching but don't yet need daily access to their coach. Includes a fully personalized training program (updated every 4 weeks), a personalized nutrition target (calories and macros, adjusted every 4 weeks based on progress), and a bi-weekly check-in via the coaching app where the client submits progress photos, weight, and a short written update, and receives written feedback and program adjustments from Marcus within 48 hours.

Best suited for clients who are self-motivated, have some prior training experience, and mainly need structure and accountability rather than constant guidance.

### Momentum Tier (Most Popular)

The core Forge Physique Coaching experience. Includes everything in Foundation, plus weekly check-ins (rather than bi-weekly), direct messaging access to Marcus via WhatsApp for day-to-day questions (responded to within 24 hours on business days), video form checks on request (client submits a short video of a lift, receives a voice-note or video breakdown), and a monthly 20-minute video call to review progress and adjust the plan in real time.

This tier is built for clients who want a coach who is genuinely present in their week-to-week decisions, not just checking in every two weeks.

### Apex Tier

The highest level of access Forge Physique Coaching offers. Includes everything in Momentum, plus near-daily check-in access (client can message daily; Marcus responds within same-day during business hours, Monday–Friday), priority scheduling for video calls (twice monthly instead of once), a fully done-for-you meal plan option (in addition to macro targets, for clients who prefer not to build their own meals), and direct access to Marcus's personal cell number for genuinely urgent training or program questions (not for general day-to-day chat — this is covered in the FAQ & Policies document).

Apex is capped at 15 active clients at any given time, specifically so that the "near-daily access" promise is realistic and never becomes an empty marketing claim.

## What Makes Forge Physique Coaching Different

- **Every program is actually custom.** No templated plans reused across clients. Every training split and nutrition target is built individually based on the client's intake data, schedule, and equipment access.
- **Direct coach access, not a team of anonymous coaches.** Every client works with Marcus directly — Forge Physique Coaching does not outsource check-ins to junior coaches as the client base grows.
- **A defined off-ramp.** Forge Physique Coaching is upfront that the goal is to eventually make itself unnecessary — clients are coached toward independence, not toward permanent dependency on the service.
- **Evidence-based, not trend-based.** Programming follows established strength and hypertrophy research, not whatever method is trending on social media that month.

## Service Area

Forge Physique Coaching is 100% online and works with clients in any timezone. Most current clients are based in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and the UAE. All coaching is delivered via a combination of a coaching app (for check-ins and program delivery), WhatsApp (for day-to-day communication), and video calls (Zoom or Google Meet, tier-dependent).

## Results Philosophy

Forge Physique Coaching does not promise specific numbers (e.g., "lose 20 lbs in 8 weeks") because individual results depend on starting point, genetics, adherence, and consistency — all of which vary client to client. What Forge Physique Coaching does promise is a program built on sound training and nutrition principles, delivered with real accountability, and adjusted continuously based on how the client's body actually responds. Typical clients who follow their program consistently see visible body composition changes within 8–12 weeks and significant transformation over a 6–12 month coaching relationship.

## A Day in the Life of a Forge Physique Client

To make the coaching relationship concrete, here is what a typical week looks like for a Momentum-tier client:

- **Monday:** Client trains per their program, logs weights/reps in the coaching app immediately after the session.
- **Wednesday:** A question comes up about substituting an exercise due to gym crowding — client sends a quick WhatsApp message, gets a response within a few hours with an approved substitution.
- **Friday:** Client submits their weekly check-in — current weight, two progress photos, training log for the week, and a short note ("Energy was low Tuesday, otherwise felt strong, hit all my target reps on squat").
- **Saturday:** Marcus reviews the check-in and responds with written feedback: confirms the plan is working, flags that Tuesday's low energy correlates with a short sleep night logged earlier in the week, and makes a small adjustment to Monday's volume for the following week.
- **Once a month:** A 20-minute video call to zoom out from the week-to-week detail and review the bigger picture — progress photos side by side, strength progression charts, and any adjustments to the overall strategy.

This cycle — train, log, check in, receive feedback, adjust — repeats continuously, which is what creates the compounding effect of real coaching versus a static program purchased once and never revisited.

## Client Outcomes (Illustrative)

Individual results vary and are never guaranteed (see Results Philosophy), but the following reflect the typical pattern Forge Physique Coaching sees across its client base:

- **Beginner clients (under 1 year of training experience):** Typically see the fastest visible changes, often noticeable body composition shifts within the first 6–8 weeks, driven by rapid neuromuscular adaptation and a first-time structured calorie target.
- **Intermediate clients (1–4 years of training experience):** Progress is slower to see visually but often the most dramatic over a 6–12 month coaching relationship, as small consistent improvements in programming and nutrition adherence compound.
- **Clients returning after a long layoff:** Often see a "re-acclimation" phase in the first 3–4 weeks where the priority is rebuilding consistent training habits and movement competency before pushing intensity, followed by steady progress from month two onward.
