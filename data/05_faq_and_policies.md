# Forge Physique Coaching — FAQ & Policies

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General

**Q: I'm a complete beginner. Is Forge Physique Coaching right for me?**
A: Yes, provided you meet the general fit criteria in the Business Overview document (age 22–45, able to commit 3 months minimum). Programs are built around your actual current level — beginners are not given the same program as an advanced lifter.

**Q: Do you coach women?**
A: No. Forge Physique Coaching currently works exclusively with men. This is a deliberate positioning choice, not a judgment on ability — Marcus's marketing, content, and client community are built specifically around a male audience.

**Q: I live outside the US. Can you still coach me?**
A: Yes. Forge Physique Coaching is 100% online and works with clients in any timezone. Current clients are based across the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and the UAE.

**Q: Do I need a gym membership?**
A: No — programs are adapted to whatever equipment you have, from a full commercial gym to a minimal home setup. See the Training & Nutrition Philosophy document for details.

**Q: How is this different from just buying a generic program online?**
A: A generic program is the same for everyone who buys it and never adjusts based on how your body actually responds. Every Forge Physique Coaching program is built individually and adjusted every 4 weeks based on your real logged data — plus you have direct access to Marcus for questions and troubleshooting, which a static program can't offer.

### Results & Timeline

**Q: How fast will I see results?**
A: Most consistent clients see visible body composition changes within 8–12 weeks. Significant transformation typically takes 6–12 months of consistent coaching. Forge Physique Coaching does not promise specific numeric results (e.g., "lose X lbs in Y weeks") because individual response varies — see the Results Philosophy section of the Business Overview document.

**Q: What if I don't see results?**
A: If you're adherent (following the program and nutrition targets consistently) and not seeing progress after a full 4-week block, this triggers a program review and adjustment — this is a normal part of the coaching process, not a failure. If adherence is the actual issue, Marcus will address that directly and honestly, per the "radical honesty" brand value.

**Q: Can I do a short-term program instead of committing to 3 months?**
A: The monthly coaching tiers require a 3-month minimum. For shorter-term needs, see the one-time consultation options in the Pricing & Payment Policy document.

### Scheduling & Access

**Q: What if I need to message Marcus outside business hours?**
A: You can message any time via the coaching app or WhatsApp, but guaranteed response times only apply within business hours (Monday–Friday, 9 AM–6 PM Central Time). See the Onboarding document for the full response-time table by tier.

**Q: What counts as "urgent" for Apex tier's personal cell access?**
A: Genuinely time-sensitive training or program issues — for example, an injury that just happened during a workout and you need immediate guidance on whether to continue, or a scheduling emergency affecting an imminent session. General day-to-day questions, even for Apex clients, should go through WhatsApp or the app, not the personal cell line. This distinction is explained clearly during Apex onboarding.

**Q: Can I switch tiers mid-coaching?**
A: Yes. Clients can upgrade at any time (prorated for the current billing period) or downgrade at their next billing cycle. Apex tier upgrades are subject to the 15-client cap and may require a short waitlist.

### Cancellation & Pausing

**Q: Can I pause my coaching instead of cancelling?**
A: Yes, one pause of up to 4 weeks is allowed per 12-month period (e.g., for travel, injury recovery, or a demanding work period), with no billing during the pause. Additional or longer pauses are considered case-by-case.

**Q: How do I cancel?**
A: After the initial 3-month minimum commitment, cancellation requires 30 days' written notice (email is sufficient) before your next billing date. There is no cancellation fee.

**Q: Can I cancel during the first 3 months?**
A: The 3-month commitment is a minimum term, not a lock-in with a penalty — but Forge Physique Coaching is upfront during the discovery call that this commitment is expected before enrollment, precisely so clients don't sign up planning to quit early. Refunds during this period follow the Refund Policy in the Pricing & Payment Policy document.

### Nutrition & Training Specifics

**Q: I have a food allergy / dietary restriction (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, etc.). Can you work with that?**
A: Yes. Dietary restrictions and preferences are collected during intake and every nutrition target is built around them from day one.

**Q: I'm training for a specific event (wedding, competition, photoshoot). Can you help with a deadline-based goal?**
A: Yes, for most goals — this is discussed on the discovery call so the timeline and approach can be set realistically. Note: Forge Physique Coaching does not currently offer competitive bodybuilding contest-prep/peak-week protocols (see the "Who We Don't Currently Work With" section of the Business Overview document).

**Q: Do you provide meal plans or just macro targets?**
A: Foundation and Momentum tiers include macro/calorie targets with full food flexibility (see Nutrition Philosophy document). A fully done-for-you meal plan is available as an added option within the Apex tier for clients who prefer not to build their own meals.

## Escalation Process

If a client has a concern, complaint, or issue that isn't resolved through normal check-in communication, the following escalation path applies:

1. **First step:** Raise the issue directly with Marcus via WhatsApp (Momentum/Apex) or the coaching app (Foundation). Most issues are resolved at this stage within the standard response window.
2. **Second step:** If unresolved after 5 business days, the client can request a dedicated video call specifically to address the concern, offered within 3 business days of the request, regardless of tier.
3. **Third step:** For billing disputes or concerns not resolved through the above, clients can email billing@forgephysiquecoaching.com directly, with a response guaranteed within 3 business days.

Forge Physique Coaching treats unresolved client concerns as a priority — the business's growth has come primarily through referrals, and an unresolved complaint is taken seriously at every stage.

## What Is NOT Included

To keep expectations clear, the following are explicitly outside the scope of any Forge Physique Coaching tier:

- Medical diagnosis, treatment, or advice of any kind
- Physical therapy or injury rehabilitation protocols (clients with active injuries are referred to a licensed PT or physician)
- Mental health counseling or therapy (clients are referred to a licensed professional if this need arises)
- In-person training sessions (Forge Physique Coaching is online-only)
- Grocery delivery, meal prep/cooking services, or supplement provision
- Guaranteed weekend or holiday response times, at any tier
- Contest prep / peak-week bodybuilding competition protocols

## Eligibility & Screening

Every applicant is screened via the application and discovery call before being accepted. Forge Physique Coaching reserves the right to decline any applicant who is not a realistic fit — including, but not limited to, applicants seeking rapid/extreme weight loss in an unsafe timeframe, applicants with an unaddressed active injury who have not sought professional clearance, or applicants who are not able to commit to the minimum engagement terms.

## Client Conduct

Forge Physique Coaching maintains a professional, respectful coaching relationship. Clients are expected to communicate respectfully; abusive or harassing communication toward Marcus is grounds for immediate termination of coaching without refund of the remaining term.

## Scope-of-Practice & Liability Disclaimer

Marcus Cole is a certified personal trainer (NASM-CPT) and certified nutrition coach (Precision Nutrition Level 1) — not a licensed physician, registered dietitian, or physical therapist. All training and nutrition guidance provided by Forge Physique Coaching is general fitness and nutrition coaching, not medical advice, and is not a substitute for consultation with a qualified healthcare provider. Clients participate in all recommended training and nutrition programs at their own risk and are responsible for obtaining medical clearance before beginning, particularly if they have a pre-existing medical condition, injury, or are pregnant.

## Privacy & Progress Photos

Progress photos and personal data submitted through the coaching app are kept private and are never shared publicly, used in marketing, or shown to any other client without the individual client's explicit written consent. Clients who wish to have their transformation featured (e.g., as a testimonial) are asked directly and can decline at any time without affecting their coaching.

## Technology Requirements

**Q: What do I actually need to use this service?**
A: A smartphone (iOS or Android) for the coaching app, an internet connection for check-ins and photo uploads, and either WhatsApp or the ability to receive app notifications, depending on your tier. Video calls (Momentum/Apex) work via Zoom or Google Meet on a phone, tablet, or computer — no special equipment needed.

**Q: What if the coaching app is down or I'm having technical issues?**
A: Report technical issues to hello@forgephysiquecoaching.com. In the rare event the app is unavailable, check-ins can be submitted temporarily via email or WhatsApp so no client falls behind on their schedule due to a technical outage.

## More Questions on Fit & Flexibility

**Q: I travel frequently for work. Can coaching still work for me?**
A: Yes. Programs are adapted for hotel gyms, bodyweight-only travel days, or whatever equipment is available on the road. This is discussed during the discovery call if travel is a regular part of your schedule.

**Q: What if I get sick or injured mid-program?**
A: Let Marcus know as soon as possible via your normal communication channel. Short-term illness typically just means a lighter week programmed in; a more significant injury may trigger a referral to a physician or PT and a program built around your restrictions in the meantime (see Scope-of-Practice Disclaimer).

**Q: Do you offer couples or group coaching?**
A: No. Forge Physique Coaching is structured around 1-on-1 coaching only, which is core to the personalized, direct-access model described throughout these documents.

**Q: I already have a nutrition coach / work with a physical therapist. Can you coordinate with them?**
A: Yes, with your written permission, Marcus is glad to coordinate programming around guidance from another professional you're already working with (e.g., PT-prescribed restrictions).

**Q: Can I do Forge Physique Coaching alongside another sport or training pursuit (e.g., running a marathon, playing a competitive sport)?**
A: Yes, this is discussed on the discovery call so your program is built to complement your other training rather than compete with it for recovery capacity.

**Q: How far in advance do I need to apply before I want to start?**
A: There's no fixed lead time — most clients complete the application-to-first-program pipeline (application, discovery call, enrollment, intake, first program) within 10–14 days total.

## Data Privacy & Security

Client data (photos, personal information, training and nutrition logs) is stored within the coaching app's secure backend and is not sold, shared with third parties for marketing purposes, or used for any purpose beyond delivering and improving the client's own coaching. Clients may request a full export or deletion of their data at any time by emailing hello@forgephysiquecoaching.com; deletion requests are processed within 14 days, except where limited billing records must be retained for legal/tax purposes.

For UK and EU-based clients, Forge Physique Coaching follows GDPR-aligned data handling principles, including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data.

## Force Majeure

In the event of circumstances outside Forge Physique Coaching's reasonable control (e.g., serious illness of the coach, natural disaster, extended power/internet infrastructure failure), response times and check-in schedules may be temporarily suspended. Affected clients are notified as soon as possible and billing is paused for any period of suspended service exceeding 5 business days.

## Policy Updates

These policies are reviewed periodically and may be updated to reflect changes in the business. Active clients are notified of any material policy change via email and app notification at least 30 days before it takes effect.
