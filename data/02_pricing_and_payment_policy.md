# Forge Physique Coaching — Pricing & Payment Policy

## Monthly Coaching Tiers

| Tier | Monthly Price | Check-In Frequency | Coach Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | $149/month | Bi-weekly | Written feedback within 48 hours |
| Momentum (Most Popular) | $249/month | Weekly | WhatsApp access, 24-hour response, monthly call |
| Apex | $449/month | Near-daily | Same-day response, 2 calls/month, personal cell for urgent issues |

All monthly tiers require a minimum 3-month initial commitment (see Minimum Commitment section below). After the initial 3 months, coaching continues month-to-month and can be cancelled with 30 days' notice.

### Why a Minimum Commitment

Physique transformation is not a 2–4 week process. A 3-month minimum reflects the actual timeline required to see and sustain real body composition change, and it protects both the client and Marcus's coaching capacity — Forge Physique Coaching intentionally does not accept clients looking for a 1-month trial-and-quit arrangement.

## Hourly & Single-Session Rates

For clients who are not ready for ongoing monthly coaching, Forge Physique Coaching offers the following à la carte options:

- **One-time program design consultation:** $85/hour — a single video call plus a one-off training program (no ongoing check-ins or adjustments included)
- **Single video form-check session:** $45 (30 minutes) — for clients who just want technique feedback on specific lifts
- **One-time nutrition consultation:** $75 (45 minutes) — calorie and macro target setting, no ongoing coaching

These hourly/single-session rates are priced higher per hour than the monthly tiers by design — ongoing monthly coaching is where Forge Physique Coaching delivers the most value and is priced accordingly to reflect that.

## How Pricing Is Set

Forge Physique Coaching's rates are benchmarked against the broader online coaching market, where standard programming-plus-check-ins packages typically run $100–$200/month, premium full-service coaching runs $200–$400+/month, and specialized high-touch coaching runs $300–$600+/month. Forge Physique Coaching's three tiers are positioned within these established market ranges, reflecting 9 years of coaching experience and a fully custom (non-templated) approach at every tier.

## Payment Methods Accepted

- Credit or debit card (via Stripe) — all monthly tiers and one-time sessions
- PayPal
- Bank transfer (for clients who prefer to pay in full for a 3, 6, or 12-month block — see Prepay Discount below)

Forge Physique Coaching does not accept cryptocurrency, checks, or cash payments.

## Billing Cycle

- Monthly tiers are billed on the same calendar date each month, starting from the client's sign-up date (e.g., a client who signs up on the 14th is billed on the 14th of each subsequent month).
- The first payment is due at the time of onboarding, before the intake call is scheduled.
- Failed payments are retried automatically after 3 days. If payment fails a second time, coaching access is paused until payment is resolved (see Payment Failure Policy below).

## Prepay Discount

Clients who prepay for a 3, 6, or 12-month block in a single payment receive a discount:

- 3-month prepay: 5% discount
- 6-month prepay: 10% discount
- 12-month prepay: 15% discount

Prepay discounts are only available for the Foundation and Momentum tiers. Apex tier is billed monthly only, due to its capped-capacity structure.

## Price Changes

Forge Physique Coaching reviews pricing annually. Existing clients are never moved to a new price mid-commitment. When rates increase, current active clients are notified at least 60 days in advance and are given the option to lock in their current rate for an additional 6 months by prepaying.

## Refund Policy

- **Within the first 7 days of the first payment:** Full refund available, no questions asked, provided the client has not yet received their first personalized training program.
- **After the first personalized program has been delivered:** No refund, as coaching work (program design, intake review) has already been completed. The client may still cancel going forward per the Cancellation Policy in the FAQ & Policies document.
- **Mid-month cancellation:** No partial-month refunds are issued. Coaching access continues through the end of the current paid billing period.

## Payment Failure Policy

If a payment fails and is not resolved within 5 business days of the second retry attempt, coaching access is paused (check-ins stop, WhatsApp access is suspended) until the outstanding balance is paid. The client's spot in Apex tier's 15-client cap is held for 14 days before being released to the waitlist.

## Currency & International Clients

All prices are listed in USD. International clients are charged in USD via Stripe or PayPal, and any currency conversion is handled by the client's card issuer or bank, not by Forge Physique Coaching. No additional international fee is charged by Forge Physique Coaching itself.

## Gift & Referral Credit

Existing clients who refer a new client that signs up for any monthly tier receive a $30 account credit applied to their next billing cycle. There is no limit to the number of referral credits a client can earn.

## Sales Tax

Coaching services are not subject to US sales tax in Texas, as personal training and coaching services fall outside the categories of tangible goods and taxable services under current Texas Comptroller guidance. Clients should not expect a sales tax line item on their invoice. International clients are responsible for any tax obligations under their own country's laws (e.g., VAT self-assessment in some EU/UK contexts), though Forge Physique Coaching does not collect or remit foreign VAT.

## Invoicing & Receipts

Every payment automatically generates a receipt emailed to the client's address on file, itemizing the tier, billing period, and amount charged. Clients who need a more formal invoice (e.g., for employer wellness reimbursement or HSA/FSA purposes in the US) can request one via billing@forgephysiquecoaching.com; these are generally not eligible for HSA/FSA reimbursement since general fitness coaching does not meet IRS medical-necessity criteria, but Forge Physique Coaching will provide documentation and leave the determination to the client's plan administrator.

## Chargeback & Payment Dispute Policy

Clients are asked to contact billing@forgephysiquecoaching.com directly with any billing concern before initiating a chargeback with their bank or card provider — the large majority of billing questions are resolved within 1–2 business days this way. An unresolved chargeback initiated without first contacting Forge Physique Coaching results in immediate suspension of coaching access pending resolution, consistent with standard service-business practice.

## Which Tier Is Right for You — A Simple Decision Guide

- **Choose Foundation if:** You have some prior training experience, you're self-motivated and consistent without needing daily accountability, and you mainly want expert programming and periodic feedback rather than constant back-and-forth.
- **Choose Momentum if:** You want a coach genuinely involved in your week — someone to message when something comes up, not just someone who reviews your numbers every two weeks. This is the right fit for the majority of clients, which is why it's Forge Physique Coaching's most-selected tier.
- **Choose Apex if:** You want the highest level of accountability available, you tend to need more frequent check-ins to stay consistent, or your goal timeline is aggressive enough that near-daily adjustment matters. Because Apex is capped at 15 clients, availability is not guaranteed — this is discussed directly on the discovery call.

Still unsure? The free 20-minute discovery call (see the Onboarding & Client Journey document) exists specifically to make this decision with Marcus rather than alone.

## Market Rate Comparison Detail

For full transparency, published 2026 market data for online physique/personal training coaching shows the following typical ranges, which informed how Forge Physique Coaching's tiers were positioned:

- Basic/templated programming only: $50–$100/month
- Standard programming plus regular check-ins: $100–$200/month
- Premium full-service coaching (nutrition + check-ins + direct access): $200–$400+/month
- High-touch specialist coaching: $300–$600+/month
- Live 1-on-1 hourly video sessions: $60–$150/hour depending on coach experience

Forge Physique Coaching's Foundation tier ($149/month) sits at the upper end of the "standard" bracket, Momentum ($249/month) sits solidly in the "premium" bracket, and Apex ($449/month) sits within the "high-touch specialist" bracket — reflecting the meaningfully higher level of access each tier provides.
